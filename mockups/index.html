Vellum · Design review · Oct 28, 2026

Mockups

A self-hosted place to upload Markdown or HTML and share it as a link. Seven screens: four for you as the owner, three for the people you send links to.

Owner

  1. Sign in

    Single-owner login. The left side doubles as the product pitch.

  2. Documents

    Your library. Drop a file anywhere on the page to publish. Each row shows a miniature of the document’s first page, its link, status and views.

  3. Publish settings

    Shown after a file is dropped. Live preview on the left; title, link, password, expiry and tracking on the right.

  4. Document detail

    The share link with a copy button, view stats, a per-section read-through chart (see where people stop reading) and version history.

Reader

  1. Markdown reader

    How a rendered .md looks to the person you sent it to: editorial typography, table of contents, reading progress, a reply button at the end, print-to-PDF.

  2. HTML document

    Your HTML shown as-is, full screen. Vellum only adds a small pill that readers can hide.

  3. Password, expired, not found

    The gate pages. Use the switch at the top to see every state.

Design system

  • Paper, not whiteWarm off-white canvas. Documents sit on a lighter sheet, like paper on a desk.
  • One vermilion button per screenThe accent is only for the main action. It never colors text, borders or icons.
  • Serif for documents, sans for the appNewsreader for titles and reading, Geist for UI, Geist Mono for links.
  • No shadowsSurfaces are separated by hairlines and tone. Two radii: 6px and pill.
  • Documents are the imageryNo illustrations or photos. The only pictures are miniatures of your own pages.
