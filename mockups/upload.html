Proposal · Prepared for Acme Corp

A faster, calmer acme.com

A twelve-week redesign of the marketing site and checkout, with a measurable goal: cut drop-off on the pricing page in half.

Where things stand

Acme's current site was built in 2019 and has grown by accretion. Forty-one templates, three button styles and two checkout flows now compete for the same visitor. Pricing-page exit rate sits at 68%, well above the 40–45% typical for B2B SaaS.

The problem is not the visual design. It is that every page asks the visitor to make a different kind of decision, in a different place, with different words.

What we will do

We propose three phases, each ending in something you can ship.
